About Us

Who We Are

We are FRC Team 11103, founded in 2025. Our team is made up of high school students from Wiregrass Ranch High School who share a passion for robotics, engineering, and STEM education.

Every year we design, build, and program a robot from scratch in just a handful weeks to compete in FIRST Robotics Competition events. Beyond the robot, we are committed to outreach and inspiring the next generation of engineers.

Our values: Gracious Professionalism, teamwork, and excellence in everything we build.
